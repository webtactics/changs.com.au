  <h3>Ingredients:</h3>
  <ul>
  <li>1 portion <a href="/products/changs-long-life-noodles/" alt="Chang's Long Life Noodles" title="Chang's Long Life Noodles" >Chang's Long Life Noodles</a></li>
  <li>1 tablespoon chicken bone broth powder*, natural flavour</li>
  <li>2 teaspoons <a href="https://www.changs.com/products/changs-tamari-light-soy-sauce/" title="Chang's Gluten Free Tamari Light Soy Sauce" alt="Chang's Gluten Free Tamari Light Soy Sauce" >Chang's Gluten Free Tamari Light Soy Sauce</a></li>
  <li>1 teaspoon <a href="https://www.changs.com/products/changs-sesame-oil/" title="Chang's Sesame Oil" alt="Chang's Sesame Oil"  >Chang's Sesame Oil</a></li>
  <li>300 ml boiling water</li>
  <li>½ green onion, finely sliced</li>
  </ul>
  <h4>Optional add-ins:</h4>
  <ul>
  <li>Cooked shredded chicken</li>
  <li>Tuna, drained</li>
  <li>Fresh coriander, roughly chopped</li>
  </ul>

  <p>*Bone broth powders and pastes vary in strength. Use the amount recommended on the packet or jar to make approximately 300 ml of broth.</p>


method: |
  <h3>Method:</h3>
  <ol>
  <li><strong>Prepare the Poaching Liquid</strong><br/>Add to a pot the chicken, ginger, green onions, peppercorns, sea salt and enough water to cover and bring to boiling point.</li>
  <li><strong>Fill the Cup</strong><br/>Place one portion of Chang’s Long Life Noodles, chicken bone broth powder, Chang’s Tamari Light Soy Sauce, Chang’s Sesame Oil and green onions into a large heatproof mug or noodle cup. Pour over enough boiling water to cover the noodles. With a fork, gently break apart the noodles and stir to combine.</li>
  <li><strong>Cover, Rest and Serve</strong><br/>Cover the mug with a lid or plate and leave for 5-7 minutes or until the noodles are tender and plump, to your liking. Stir well and enjoy immediately.</li>
  </ol>

  <p>If the broth has cooled too much, microwave for 1 minute, or until hot, before serving.</p>
