  <h3>Ingredients:</h3>
  <h4>Chicken:</h4>
  <ul>
  <li>3 chicken Maryland pieces (drumstick and thigh)</li>
  <li>5-6 cups water</li>
  <li>4 slices fresh ginger</li>
  <li>2 green onions, cut into 5 cm lengths and lightly smashed</li>
  <li>10 whole peppercorns</li>
  <li>1 teaspoon sea salt</li>
  </ul>
  <h4>Ginger Scallion Sauce:</h4>
  <ul>
  <li>2 tablespoons vegetable oil</li>
  <li>3 green onions, finely sliced</li>
  <li>1 tablespoon minced ginger</li>
  <li>1 teaspoon chilli flakes (optional to add chilli oil)</li>
  <li>2 tablespoons <a href="https://www.changs.com/products/changs-tamari-light-soy-sauce/" title="Chang's Gluten Free Tamari Light Soy Sauce" alt="Chang's Gluten Free Tamari Light Soy Sauce" >Chang's Gluten Free Tamari Light Soy Sauce</a></li>
  <li>1 tablespoon <a href="https://www.changs.com/products/changs-original-oyster-sauce/" title="Chang's Original Oyster Sauce" alt="Chang's Original Oyster Sauce" target="_blank">Chang's Original Oyster Sauce</a></li>
  <li>¾ cup reserved chicken poaching liquid</li>
  <li>Salt and pepper, to taste</li>
  <li>2 teaspoons <a href="https://www.changs.com/products/changs-sesame-oil/" title="Chang's Sesame Oil" alt="Chang's Sesame Oil" target="_blank" >Chang's Sesame Oil</a></li>
  </ul>
  <h4>To Serve:</h4>
  <ul>
  <li>1 packet <a href="https://www.changs.com/products/changs-vermicelli-rice-noodles/" title="Chang's Vermicelli Rice Noodles" alt="Chang's Vermicelli Rice Noodles" target="_blank">Chang's Vermicelli Rice Noodles</a></li>
  </ul>
method: |
  <h3>Method:</h3>
  <ol>
  <li><strong>Prepare the Poaching Liquid</strong><br/>Add to a pot the chicken, ginger, green onions, peppercorns, sea salt and enough water to cover and bring to boiling point.</li>
  <li><strong>Poach the Chicken</strong><br/>Once boiling, reduce the heat, cover and gently simmer for 20 minutes.</li>
  <li><strong>Rest the Chicken and Prepare the Noodles</strong><br/>Turn off the heat, cover the pot and leave the chicken to rest in the poaching liquid for a further 10–15 minutes.<br/>To check that it is cooked, insert a chopstick or skewer into the thickest part of a thigh.<br/>The chicken is ready when the juices run clear.<br/>Meanwhile, place the rice vermicelli noodles in a heatproof bowl and cover completely with boiling water.<br/>Leave for 3–4 minutes, or until tender, then drain well.</li>
  <li><strong>Shred the Chicken</strong><br/>Remove the chicken from the poaching liquid and reserve ¾ of a cup of the cooking liquid. Once cool enough to handle, shred or slice the chicken.</li>
  <li><strong>Cook the Ginger Scallion Sauce</strong><br/>While the chicken is cooling, add the vegetable oil to a small pot over medium heat.<br/>Add the green onions, ginger and chilli flakes, then cook for 1 minute until fragrant.<br/>Stir in Chang’s Tamari Light Soy Sauce, Chang’s Oyster Sauce, Chang’s Sesame Oil and the reserved poaching liquid.<br/>Simmer for a few minutes.</li>
  <li><strong>Serve</strong><br/>Arrange the noodles on a serving platter and place the chicken over the top.<br/>Spoon the warm ginger scallion sauce generously over the chicken and noodles. <br/>Alternatively, coat the chicken with the sauce in a separate bowl and allow it to stand for 1–2 minutes to absorb the flavours before arranging it over the noodles.<br/>Serve immediately.</li>
  </ol>
